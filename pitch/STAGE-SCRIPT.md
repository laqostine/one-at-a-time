# One at a time — 2-minute stage script

**0:00 Hook (15 s)**
"On r/deaf someone wrote: I smiled and agreed, and I have no idea what I signed up for. That happens at every family table. About 50 million people in the EU report trouble hearing, and the family table is the number one place they want to hear."

**0:15 Idea (15 s)**
"Every tool puts the burden on the deaf person. We built the other side. It's called One at a time. One phone on the table listens. Other phones can join with a 4-letter code and become lamps."

**0:30 Demo: play the video or the live link (45 s)**
- One sentence at a time, with who said it and how.
- Someone asks you a question: the whole screen turns amber. One tap, Yes, and every phone sees it.
- Looked away? Three lines tell you what you missed.
- The plans with their reasons, a map of the conversation, and every table remembered with place and date.

**1:15 How it works (25 s)**
"Deepgram does live speech and tells speakers apart. Claude runs a fast gate under a second on every line, a clerk that keeps the plans, and the catch-up. And we're honest: no microphone can hear two people at once. That's exactly why the phones turn amber — the table does its part."

**1:40 Close (20 s)**
"Nothing to wear. Nothing to install. Open table.akilion.ai, tap I'm reading, put the phone down. One at a time."
