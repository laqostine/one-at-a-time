### 1. Top 8 Concrete Pains DHH People Report in Group Conversations

#### **1. Dinner Table Syndrome (DTS) & Cumulative Social Isolation**
* **Description:** In informal, multi-speaker settings (family dinners, social 
gatherings, work lunches), rapid conversational shifts, overlapping speech, and 
lack of visual accommodations leave DHH individuals completely excluded [1-3]. 
Over time, this leads to profound emotional exhaustion and loss of incidental 
social knowledge [2, 4, 5].
* **Verbatim Reddit Quote:** *"DTS - Dinner Table Syndrome. Deaf people in 
hearing families are too familiar with this, myself included. It’s simply too 
much work to follow along. Thats why you feel like you could sleep for a week, 
we have to work much harder than a hearing person to understand verbal 
conversations."* — user `little_turkey` on `r/deaf` [5].
* **Research Citation:** Meek (2020) defines Dinner Table Syndrome as the 
systemic exclusion of deaf individuals from mealtime dialogues due to hearing 
turn-taking mechanisms, leaving DHH individuals feeling "loved, yet 
disconnected" [1].

---

#### **2. "Whiplash" Speaker Identification & Loss of Spatial Localization**
* **Description:** Standard captions rarely identify who is talking in real time
or where they are located [6, 7]. Hearing peers locate speakers instantly using 
binaural audio, whereas DHH users must visually scan the room, experiencing 
physical "whiplash" and missing the first few seconds of every turn [7-9].
* **Verbatim Reddit Quote:** *"Today I just could not keep up with the 
conversation. Banter was going back and forth and I was getting whiplash trying 
to identify who was talking and focus on what they were saying."* — user 
`Educational-Hat-7155` on `r/deaf` [8].
* **Research Citation:** SpeechCompass (2025) and Glasser et al. (RTTD-ID, 2019)
document that the inability to locate and distinguish speakers is a top 
complaint among caption users, leading to viewing isolation and severe context 
fragmentation [9, 10].

---

#### **3. Overlapping Speech & Crosstalk Distortion**
* **Description:** When multiple participants talk simultaneously, Automatic 
Speech Recognition (ASR) engines drop words, merge utterances, or fail 
completely [11, 12]. Unable to decipher crosstalk, DHH users are forced to 
disengage [12].
* **Verbatim Reddit Quote:** *"I work in marketing and we have these weekly team
meetings where everyone just talks over each other. By the end I'm exhausted 
from trying to lipread four different people and honestly I've started just 
nodding along even when I have no idea what was decided."* — user 
`Main_Lengthiness_606` on `r/deaf` [13, 14].
* **Research Citation:** McDonnell et al. (CHI 2023) highlight overlapping 
speech as a major unaddressed access barrier in videoconferencing that causes 
DHH users to pull away emotionally or physically from group interactions [12].

---

#### **4. Caption Latency, Reaction Disconnect, & Obsolete Interjections**
* **Description:** Live ASR captions carry a 1–2 second delay (and TV captions 
up to 7–12 seconds) [15]. This lag creates a confusing disconnect between visual
room reactions (laughter/cheering) and text, while causing DHH interjections to 
arrive after the conversation has already moved on [15-17].
* **Verbatim Reddit Quote:** *"I really want to contribute more but I feel like 
I'm always three steps behind everyone else."* — user `Main_Lengthiness_606` on 
`r/deaf` [13].
* **Research Citation:** TruthBytes (2025) and Cognitive & Contextual Dynamics 
establish that processing lag impairs conversational footing, causing DHH users 
to accidentally interrupt others or offer points that are already obsolete [16, 
17].

---

#### **5. Visual Split-Attention & Extreme Reading Fatigue**
* **Description:** DHH users must continuously split their gaze between speaker 
faces (lip-reading), presentation slides/equipment, and scrolling caption text 
[18, 19]. This continuous saccadic eye jumping imposes a heavy cognitive 
workload and extreme mental exhaustion [18, 20].
* **Verbatim Reddit Quote:** *"The absolute exhaustion from trying to keep up, 
engage, be sociable, be professional, and still deliver your work is a lot... 
You can actually get a headache and be physically tired from it."* — user 
`Crazycatladyanddave` on `r/deaf` [14, 21].
* **Research Citation:** The ARRAE Platform Study (2026) and From Barriers to 
Accessible Design (2026) show that visual split-attention competes for the same 
cognitive resources needed for language processing, causing teleconference 
fatigue [18, 20].

---

#### **6. Missing Non-Speech Information (NSI), Laughter, Prosody, & Tone**
* **Description:** Verbatim ASR converts expressive human speech into flat, 
unformatted text blocks, stripping away prosody (pitch, volume, sarcasm) and 
environmental sounds (laughter, applause, side comments) [22-24].
* **Verbatim Reddit Quote:** *"Not hearing the whole joke or the punchline in 
the moment at a table full of people so everyone is laughing but you so you have
to either ignore it, fake laugh or ask them to repeat it while everyone is 
silent. Totally awkward..."* — user `Icy_Homework_2477` on `r/hardofhearing` 
[25, 26].
* **Research Citation:** EEG studies (PMC7040021) and May et al. (2025) 
demonstrate that standard text captions (<4% NSI coverage) fail to trigger the 
automatic neural emotional responses elicited by acoustic audio cues [22, 24, 
27].

---

#### **7. Social Stigma & The Dismissive "Never Mind" Brush-Off**
* **Description:** Halting group conversations to ask for repetitions creates 
social discomfort and perceived burden [4, 13]. DHH individuals are frequently 
dismissed with *"Never mind, it wasn't important,"* which deepens social 
marginalization [28-30].
* **Verbatim Reddit Quote:** *"Asking people to repeat themselves a couple times
and still not totally hearing or understanding them and then they say 'Never 
mind' or 'It's not important'."* — user `HerNameIsRio805` on `r/hardofhearing` 
[28, 29].
* **Research Citation:** Wang & Piper (2018) and ASLdeafined document the social
stigma and emotional damage caused when hearing conversation partners refuse to 
adapt and dismiss DHH access requests [4, 31].

---

#### **8. Post-Hoc Summary Compression Dropping Live Objections & Debates**
* **Description:** Post-meeting AI summaries compress meetings into final 
conclusions, completely erasing intermediate disagreements, live objections, or 
unstated nuances that occurred while a decision was actively forming [32, 33].
* **Verbatim Reddit Quote:** *"I will join meetings and participate as much as 
possible, but I have a 'rule' where if I need to be involved in a project then 
somebody has to email me the details otherwise it's on them if I miss 
something."* — user `duskcat101` on `r/deaf` [14, 34].
* **Research Citation:** TruthBytes (2025) shows that AI meeting summaries 
support post-hoc recall rather than live access; they discard active debate and 
objections, leaving DHH users unable to intervene while decisions are still open
[32, 33].

| **Google Live Transcribe** | Low-latency mobile ASR text display on phone

### 3. The Single Most Underserved, Hackathon-Feasible Pain (12-Hour Build on 
Laptop/Phone Only)

#### **Target Pain: Real-Time Live Objection & Decision-History Buffer (Pain #8 
+ Pain #4)**
* **Why it's the most underserved:** Current tools either present continuous 
scrolling text (which causes whiplash and reading fatigue) [20, 39] or 
post-meeting AI summaries (which arrive *after* the meeting ends, when decisions
are already finalized and objections are dropped) [32, 33]. No existing software
allows a DHH participant during an active live meeting to discreetly catch up on
unresolved objections or live debates without asking people to repeat themselves
[17].

---

#### **12-Hour Software Solution: `ObjectionBuffer` (Discreet In-Meeting Context
Recovery Panel)**

* **Form Factor:** Software-only Chrome Extension or React/Electron floating 
widget running alongside Zoom, Teams, or browser audio (zero external hardware, 
smart glasses, or multi-mic arrays needed).
* **Technical Architecture:**
  1. **Sliding Audio/Text Buffer:** Captures the live ASR caption stream into a 
rolling 3-minute sliding window memory array.
  2. **Discreet 1-Click "Catch Me Up" Trigger:** When the DHH user looks back at
the screen after looking away or taking notes, clicking a discreet side button 
(or pressing `Cmd+Shift+C`) triggers a fast LLM prompt (e.g., Gemini 1.5 Flash).
  3. **Targeted Extraction Prompt:** The prompt extracts strictly 3 structured 
bullets:
     * **Current Core Topic / Decision in Progress**
     * **Live Objections / Dissenting Points:** Who raised a question or 
objection that hasn't been resolved?
     * **Who Spoke Last & Direct Questions:** Was a direct question asked to the
user?
  4. **Static Non-Intrusive Card:** Displays a static 3-bullet floating card for
10 seconds (no scrolling, no flickering, no layout jitter) before automatically 
fading out.

* **Why Achievable in 12 Hours:** 
  * Uses standard Web Speech API / browser DOM transcript listeners.
  * Connects directly to an LLM REST API with a focused prompt template.
  * Pure front-end UI overlay (HTML/Tailwind/React)—no custom hardware, audio 
signal DSP, or complex 3D rendering required.

* **Direct Impact:** Enables DHH users to recover missed context and intervene 
while a decision is actively forming, eliminating the embarrassment of asking 
*"What did I miss?"* and overriding the "Never mind" brush-off [16, 17, 28].
